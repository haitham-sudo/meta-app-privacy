Privacy Policy

A personal project by Haitham Sankari
Last updated: 27 September 2026

1. Who operates this app

This software application ("the App") is a personal project developed and operated by Haitham Sankari ("I", "me"), an individual developer. The App connects to the Meta (Facebook and Instagram) Marketing API to create, manage and report on advertising campaigns in ad accounts I am authorised to manage. It is used only by me and is not offered to the public.

2. What data the App accesses

  • Advertising account data for ad accounts I am authorised to manage: campaigns, ad sets, ads, budgets, schedules, targeting settings and creative assets (images and captions).
  • Aggregated performance metrics for those campaigns (for example impressions, reach, clicks and spend).
  • Basic details of Facebook Pages and linked Instagram accounts that I am authorised to advertise on behalf of.

The App does not collect personal data from members of the public, does not read private messages, and does not access personal profile information beyond my own name and ID as the authorising user.

3. How the data is used

  • To create and manage advertising campaigns on behalf of the owners of those ad accounts.
  • To produce performance reports and dashboards for the owners of those ad accounts.
  • To maintain a historical archive of campaign settings and performance data for future reference and analysis.

4. Sharing

I do not sell data. Campaign data and performance reports are shared only with the owner of the relevant ad account (and people they authorise), and with service providers that host the App's systems under confidentiality obligations.

5. Storage and security

Data is stored in access-controlled systems. Access credentials are kept private and are never shared publicly.

6. Retention and deletion

Campaign data retrieved by the App (campaign settings and aggregated performance metrics) is kept in a long-term archive for future reference and analysis, and may be retained indefinitely. This archive contains advertising data only, not personal data about members of the public.

If you believe the App holds personal data about you, or you own an ad account and have withdrawn my authorisation to manage it, you can contact me at the address below to request deletion. I will respond within 30 days.
